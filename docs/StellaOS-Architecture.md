Stella OS Architecture
Version: 1.0 Candidate

Stella OS is a constitutional cognitive architecture designed to support reflective dialogue while preserving human sovereignty, explainability, traceability, and governance.

The system is organized into layered cognitive domains coordinated through a unified ConstitutionalResponsePipeline.

Architectural Principles
Human Sovereignty
The user remains the final authority regarding identity, meaning, and interpretation.

Constitutional Governance
All cognitive outputs must pass constitutional constraints and avoid authority claims, certainty claims, and destiny framing.

Explainability
Major cognitive outputs should be traceable to identifiable subsystems.

Layer Separation
Identity, Meaning, Wisdom, Presence, and Attunement remain independent domains coordinated through orchestration rather than duplication.
